# Country aliases

**Format: table** — columns `token`, `iso`, `notes` (notes optional).

Two-letter tokens that name a country but are not its ISO 3166 code.

| token | iso | notes                                 |
|-------|-----|---------------------------------------|
| UK    | GB  | United Kingdom (ISO is GB)            |
| EL    | GR  | Greece (EL is the EU/VAT code)        |
